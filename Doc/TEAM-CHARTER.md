# Northstar Sprint: Team Charter

## 1. Team Operations & Communication
* **Primary Communication Channel:** WhatsApp
* **Core Working Hours:** Monday – Thursday, 19:00 – 21:00 EAT
* **Daily Asynchronous Updates:** 09:00 EAT and 22:00 EAT
* **Response SLA:** All internal tickets and communications must be acknowledged and responded to within 24 hours of submission.

## 2. Task Management & Workflow
* **Task Assignment & Acceptance:** Tasks will be delegated via the project board, and team members must explicitly acknowledge and accept their assignments prior to beginning work.
* **Workflow Board Statuses:** To-Do -> In-Progress -> Done
* **Definition of Done (DoD):** Every assigned task must include a single, clear statement defining its specific success criteria before it can be considered complete.
* **Internal Deadline:** All tasks must be completed internally by **Thursday at 23:59 EAT** to ensure ample time for review prior to the final Friday sprint submission.

## 3. Project Guidelines & Data Privacy
* **Fictional Data Policy:** All data utilized for writing code and developing the project must be strictly fictional. The use of real-world or sensitive data is explicitly prohibited.

## 4. Accountability & Conflict Resolution
* **Task Impediments:** If a team member anticipates being unable to complete an assigned task, they must proactively notify the rest of the group immediately.
* **Breach of Responsibilities:** Should a member fail to complete their tasks without prior notification to the team, the matter will be formally escalated to the PLP Administrators via official communication channels.
* **Conflict Resolution:** 
  * *Minor Conflicts:* Team members will first attempt to resolve the issue internally through open discussion to reach a mutual agreement. 
  * *Major Conflicts:* Unresolved disputes or major offenses will be escalated directly to the PLP team.
